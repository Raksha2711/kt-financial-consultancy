Questions Clients Ask Most

Plain answers to common tax, GST, TDS and compliance questions — no jargon.

Income Tax
Who is required to file an Income Tax Return in India? +
Any individual with gross total income above the basic exemption limit (₹2.5 lakh under old regime / ₹3 lakh under new regime for FY 2025-26), residents with foreign assets, anyone claiming a refund, and businesses/professionals as per Section 139 must file an ITR.
What is the difference between the old and new tax regimes? +
The new regime offers lower slab rates but removes most deductions (80C, 80D, HRA etc.). The old regime keeps higher rates but allows full deductions. The right choice depends on your investments, home loan interest and salary structure — we run a side-by-side computation for every client before recommending.
What happens if I miss the ITR due date? +
A belated return can be filed until 31 December of the assessment year with a late fee under Section 234F (up to ₹5,000) plus interest under Section 234A. Losses (except house property loss) cannot be carried forward if the return is belated.
What income tax slab rates apply for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)? +
Under the New Regime (default): up to ₹3 lakh — nil; ₹3–7 lakh — 5%; ₹7–10 lakh — 10%; ₹10–12 lakh — 15%; ₹12–15 lakh — 20%; above ₹15 lakh — 30%. A rebate under Section 87A makes tax nil up to ₹12 lakh of income under the new regime. Under the Old Regime: up to ₹2.5 lakh — nil; ₹2.5–5 lakh — 5%; ₹5–10 lakh — 20%; above ₹10 lakh — 30%.
GST
What is the turnover threshold for GST registration? +
₹40 lakh for goods (₹20 lakh in special-category states) and ₹20 lakh for services. Inter-state suppliers, e-commerce operators and casual taxable persons must register irrespective of turnover.
Can I claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on all purchases? +
Only on purchases used for business, where the supplier has filed GSTR-1 and the invoice appears in your GSTR-2B, and where ITC is not blocked under Section 17(5) — e.g. motor vehicles for personal use, food & beverages, club memberships.
What are GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C? +
GSTR-9 is the annual return summarising the year's outward & inward supplies and tax paid. GSTR-9C is a self-certified reconciliation statement, mandatory for taxpayers with turnover above ₹5 crore. Both are due by 31 December following the financial year.
What is the GST late fee for delayed GSTR-3B filing? +
₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST) for taxpayers with tax liability, subject to a maximum of ₹10,000. For NIL returns, the late fee is ₹20 per day (₹10 CGST + ₹10 SGST). Additionally, interest @18% per annum applies on the unpaid tax from the due date.
TDS / TCS
When is TDS required to be deducted by a business? +
When making payments above specified thresholds — salary (Sec 192), contractor payments (194C: ₹30,000/single or ₹1 lakh/annual), rent (194I: ₹2.4 lakh/year), professional fees (194J: ₹30,000/year), commission (194H: ₹15,000/year), etc. The deductor must hold a TAN.
What are the consequences of late TDS deposit? +
Interest @ 1% per month from date of deductibility to actual deduction, and @ 1.5% per month from deduction to actual deposit. Plus late-filing fees under Section 234E (₹200/day) for delayed TDS returns, subject to a maximum of the TDS amount.
When must TDS be deposited to the government? +
By the 7th of the following month for all deductions (except March — deadline is 30th April). For salary TDS deducted in March, deposit is also due by 30th April. TDS must be paid using Challan 281 at authorised bank branches or online via NSDL/TIN 2.0.
Company & ROC Compliance
What annual ROC filings are mandatory for a Private Limited Company? +
Form AOC-4 (financial statements) within 30 days of AGM and Form MGT-7A (annual return for small companies) within 60 days of AGM. DIR-3 KYC for every director by 30 September each year. Non-filing attracts daily penalties of ₹100–₹200 per day per form.
What is the minimum capital required to start a Private Limited Company? +
There is no minimum paid-up capital requirement since the Companies (Amendment) Act, 2015. You can incorporate with as little as ₹1 lakh authorised capital. Most founders start with ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh authorised capital depending on their business needs.
What is the difference between a Private Limited Company and an LLP? +
A Pvt Ltd Company has stricter compliance (board meetings, statutory audit mandatory), better funding credibility, and separate legal identity. An LLP has fewer compliance requirements (audit only above ₹40 lakh turnover / ₹25 lakh capital), lower setup cost, and partners are taxed individually. For most small consultancies and service firms, LLP is simpler and cheaper.
