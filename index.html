What I Do
Services Offered
01 / GST

GST Compliance

Registration, monthly & quarterly returns (GSTR-1, 3B), annual returns (9/9C), and 2A/2B reconciliation.

02 / INCOME TAX

Income Tax Filing

ITR filing for individuals, firms, and companies. Tax planning, advance tax computation, and form 26AS review.

03 / TDS

TDS & TCS Compliance

Quarterly TDS returns (24Q, 26Q, 27Q), correction statements, challan reconciliation, and lower deduction certificates.

04 / BOOKS

Bookkeeping & Accounting

Monthly and quarterly accounting using Tally or Zoho Books, keeping your records clean and audit-ready year-round.

05 / PAYROLL

Payroll & Statutory

Payroll processing along with PF, ESI, and Professional Tax compliance to keep your team payments on track.

06 / ROC

ROC & Company Compliance

Company and LLP incorporation support, annual ROC filings, and maintaining your entity's good standing.

07 / ADVISORY

08 / NOTICES

Notice & Scrutiny Support

09 / VIRTUAL CFO

Virtual CFO Services

Ongoing financial oversight, reporting, and strategic guidance for startups and growing small businesses.
